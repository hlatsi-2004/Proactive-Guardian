# Proactive Guardian

**A safety system that sees danger coming — before it happens.**

**Live demo:** [https://home-safety-angel.lovable.app](https://home-safety-angel.lovable.app)

---

## What Is This?

Most safety systems wait until something goes wrong. A baby monitor shows you what already happened. A smoke alarm screams after the fire starts. A medical alert button only works if you can still press it.

Every system we have is **reactive** — a witness to tragedy, never a preventer of it.

**Proactive Guardian takes a different approach.**

Instead of just detecting a child near a hazard, it **perceives danger the way a human would** — understanding posture, movement, and surroundings to judge whether a situation is truly dangerous. Then it warns the caregiver with the right level of urgency — calm when it can be, urgent when it must be.

---

## What It Does

| **The System...** | **In Plain Terms** |
| :--- | :--- |
| Watches a room | Uses a camera to see what's happening |
| Understands movement | Knows if a child is standing, walking, leaning, or reaching |
| Reads the situation | Combines distance, speed, and posture to judge risk |
| Decides if it matters | Stays silent when nothing is wrong, alerts when it counts |
| Warns the caregiver | Speaks in a calm, firm, or critical voice depending on danger |

---

## The Five Levels of Danger

The system doesn't just say "danger" or "safe." It understands that risk is a spectrum.

| **Level** | **What's Happening** | **What the System Does** |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **L1** | Child is safe | Silent monitoring |
| **L2** | Child is near a hazard | Watches silently, logs the moment |
| **L3** | Child is moving toward danger | Calm voice: *"Your child is approaching the hot stove."* |
| **L4** | Child is close and posture shows intent | Firm voice: *"Move them away now."* |
| **L5** | Contact is imminent | Critical alert: red pulse, firm voice, phone notification |

**The point:** the system only speaks when it needs to. That's what makes it trustworthy.

## How It Works (Without the Jargon)

1. **A camera watches the room.**
2. **Software finds the child and understands their body position.**
3. **The system measures how close they are to danger and how fast they're moving toward it.**
4. **A trained model decides the risk level — from 1 to 5.**
5. **If the risk is serious, the system speaks.**

**All of this happens on the device itself.** No video is sent to the cloud. No recordings are stored. Privacy is built in, not added later.

---

## Why It Matters

Childhood home injuries — falls, burns, drowning — are preventable. Yet they still happen every day, in ordinary homes, to ordinary families.

Parents try to watch their children closely. But they can't watch every second. A single moment of distraction can lead to tragedy.

**Proactive Guardian exists to give those moments back.**

## What's Next

This project started with child safety. But the same perception engine can protect anyone:

- **Elderly people** — detecting falls before they happen
- **Homeowners** — spotting intruders by behaviour, not just motion
- **Healthcare** — monitoring vulnerable patients
- **Public spaces** — schools, hospitals, transport hubs

The goal is bigger than a baby monitor. It's a **guardian** — for every family, in every home, against every danger.
